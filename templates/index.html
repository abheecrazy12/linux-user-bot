Review the exact command before it runs. Click Confirm to execute or Cancel to abort.

Multi-Server Support

Each browser tab / phone session is completely independent. Open the app on two devices and connect each to a different server — they won't interfere. Credentials are stored in your encrypted browser session cookie and never saved to the server's disk.
